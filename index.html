①   THE WHY

No dramatic story. Just commitment.

Running has become a major part of my life over the last few years — I train consistently each week and regularly cover half marathon distances. London has been a long term goal, and combining it with meaningful fundraising feels like the right way to take it on.

If I'm going to run 26.2 miles, I'd rather do it for a cause where the effort makes a genuine impact than simply for myself. This page exists to back that up — every training mile logged, every fundraising effort tracked, in public for everyone to see, in real time.

★ the cause ★
RUNNING FOR

Whizz Kidz

I chose Whizz Kidz from a shortlist of children's charities because the impact is concrete. Around 75,000 young people in the UK need a wheelchair to be mobile, and Whizz Kidz exists to give them the equipment, skills and confidence to go further — at home, at school, at work and beyond.

I'm a dad. If I'm going to run 26.2 miles, I want every pound raised to do something I can actually point at. £3,500 is what I've committed to, and every donation gets Whizz Kidz closer to their vision of a society in which every young wheelchair user is mobile, enabled and included.
